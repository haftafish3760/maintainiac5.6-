MAINTAINIAC GPS-ASSISTED TRIP-TRACKING MASTER REPORT
=====================================================

Purpose
-------
This is one complete working report for Maintainiac's GPS-assisted trip-
tracking system, its Dashboard requirements, its security model, and the
remaining engineering work. It is written as a copy-paste document for product
planning and engineering review.

This report is not source code. It is intentionally exempt from the normal
500-line source-file limit. It is the single authoritative report requested by
the owner; the earlier split planning files are supporting notes only.

Current truthfulness statement
------------------------------
Maintainiac has a meaningful code-level foundation and substantial focused test
coverage. The current complete trip-tracking QA gate is green at 1,990 tests,
and the required post-repair 100,000-scenario deterministic stress runs are
green. It is still NOT honestly describable as field-verified, commercially
ready, or production-ready. It needs reachable final Dashboard safety
projections plus Android/iPhone controlled physical-device and repeated road
testing.

The product being built
-----------------------
Maintainiac is a local-first commercial record-keeping system for people who
use a vehicle in their work: gig/delivery drivers, contractors, lawn-care and
route businesses, plumbers, electricians, HVAC technicians, service workers,
home-health workers, non-emergency transport businesses, and similar users.

It must be useful for all of these users:

- A person who manually enters beginning and ending odometer readings only.
- A person who uses optional GPS assistance while actively working.
- A person who wants optional Bluetooth vehicle identification.
- A person who has one vehicle, several vehicles, one work profile, or many.
- A person whose work is all business, all personal, or mixed.
- A person who drives an irregular route or the same route every week.
- A person with weak signal, poor GPS conditions, background restrictions,
  low battery, an old phone, no Bluetooth, no maps, or no internet.

The system must help a hurried user avoid lost workdays and recording mistakes
without becoming invasive, confusing, or automatic in ways that take ownership
away from the user.

Core philosophy
---------------
Maintainiac is an evidence engine, not an assumption engine.

GPS, Bluetooth, activity recognition, schedules, maps, receipts, invoices,
jobs, notes, historical patterns, and user entries are evidence sources. They
may help reconstruct a day. They do not become a confirmed business record
without user review and approval, unless a future immutable product rule
explicitly says otherwise.

The final authority for a business record is the user. The user can accept,
correct, classify, defer, reject, or ignore a proposal. Automation exists to
reduce effort, not to silently take control.

Immutable product rules
-----------------------
1. The physical vehicle odometer is canonical mileage truth.
2. GPS is advisory evidence; it does not replace an official odometer reading.
3. Bluetooth is advisory vehicle-identification evidence; it does not prove
   business purpose or silently change the confirmed vehicle of a trip.
4. Maps and Mapbox are optional presentation/routing adapters only. They never
   own GPS truth, mileage truth, trip state, stop confirmation, invoice state,
   or business records.
5. Tracking proposes. The user and the appropriate review/TripLog flow confirm.
6. Manual record keeping remains usable without GPS, Bluetooth, Maps, internet,
   cloud services, Firebase, AI, background permission, or motion sensors.
7. Only one active local tracking session may exist at a time.
8. Session transitions are deterministic, durable, and recoverable after crash,
   restart, process death, operating-system kill, backgrounding, or reboot.
9. Duplicate starts, callbacks, samples, events, revisions, completion
   attempts, handoffs, and distance accumulation must be rejected or harmless.
10. Ordering/revision evidence must be preserved separately from wall-clock
    display time so clock changes cannot corrupt record ordering.
11. Cached location is never a valid current start merely because it exists.
12. The app never fabricates distance across a pause, rejected evidence, or a
    signal gap.
13. Accepted measured distance, estimated-gap distance, and rejected distance
    are separate facts.
14. Walking never creates vehicle mileage.
15. Stationary behavior never automatically finalizes a trip or workday.
16. Confirmed vehicle, profile, odometer, trip, allocation, or business history
    is never silently rewritten by GPS, Bluetooth, Maps, cloud, or automation.
17. Every automated proposal includes evidence, a human explanation, expected
    result if accepted, decision state, and audit history.
18. Uncertain/conflicting evidence is communicated honestly; it is never shown
    as certainty.
19. Privacy, security, data minimization, and local-first behavior apply from
    the first release, not later.
20. A remote counter, backup, map, GPS stream, or AI result cannot set local
    odometer truth or silently finalize a business record.

What has been implemented and verified so far
---------------------------------------------

ODOMETER TRUTH AND TENTH-MILE SUPPORT

Implemented:

- Odometer history is kept conceptually separate from GPS distance.
- Start/end odometer entries support optional tenths of a mile.
- A user is not forced to enter a tenth because many modern physical odometers
  display only whole miles.
- Exact tenths are retained as integers where known. This avoids floating-point
  drift and supports accurate 0.1-mile display.
- Legacy whole-mile compatibility fields are derived from exact tenths instead
  of separately rounded. This prevents recovered records from disagreeing about
  the same mileage value.
- A confirmed trip, vehicle, work profile, or odometer event cannot be silently
  rewritten by GPS estimates, calibration, recovery, or remote data.

Verified focused evidence:

- Odometer integrity suite: 146 passing tests at the prior checkpoint.
- Exact-tenth calibration/reconciliation suite: 64 passing tests after a
  compatibility repair.
- Settings/odometer policy suite: 36 passing tests after consent-policy repair.

Current user-facing lower-reading behavior represented in code:

- If end mileage is lower than the active workday start, the workday remains
  open. The app does not close the day, change the odometer, or create a fake
  trip.
- A durable pending review record can preserve the entered lower reading and
  its reason.
- The lower-reading correction panel offers these reasons:
  * Correct the entered reading.
  * Choose a different vehicle.
  * This is a backdated receipt or trip.
  * Review the previous odometer entry.
  * The odometer was repaired or replaced.
  * The odometer completed a rollover.
  * The distance unit setting changed.
  * I am not sure yet.
- “Typed wrong” and “wrong vehicle” tell the user to retry without saving the
  lower value as official mileage.
- “Backdated receipt/trip” retains a review item rather than rolling the current
  odometer backward.
- Prior-entry error, replacement, rollover, and unit change are intended to use
  an audited correction flow that keeps original history.
- “I am not sure” retains an unresolved review item instead of blocking every
  other part of the workday.

Important incomplete work in this area:

- The final visible Dashboard placement of odometer anomaly/calibration alerts
  is not currently proven. Two old tests target a widget explicitly marked as
  retired and never rendered. The logic exists, but a user cannot be told an
  alert is visible until that alert is wired into the current Dashboard design.
- The full lower-reading flow needs direct end-to-end widget and later device
  proof: selecting each reason, backing out, retrying, saving a review, viewing
  it later, correction audit, process restart, and End Day continuation.

ODOMETER DISCREPANCY PREVENTION PLAN

The app should prevent errors through several layers, never through a simplistic
“block the user and offer no explanation” approach.

Layer 1: direct continuity.

- Compare entered end to active workday/context start.
- Compare new start to prior confirmed same-vehicle ending.
- Keep different vehicles completely separate.
- Use exact tenths before display rounding.
- Lower values require review, not silent correction.

Layer 2: elapsed-time plausibility.

- Calculate a soft average-mile-per-working-hour signal using workday duration.
- Consider recorded stops/dwell time if available.
- An extremely high apparent average is a review prompt, not an accusation:
  highway routes, emergencies, incomplete stops, and unusual days happen.
- This does not automatically block a truthful entry.

Layer 3: optional local historical pattern.

- Only for a user who explicitly enables local mileage-pattern help.
- Use confirmed, reviewed, same-vehicle days only.
- Exclude corrupt records, other vehicles, future confirmations, vacation/out
  of-town markers, too few days, and outliers that distort normal history.
- Optionally compare same weekdays or confirmed route types.
- Explain “unusually high/low compared to your reviewed history”; do not imply
  the user did anything wrong and do not alter the value.

Layer 4: GPS-assisted comparison.

- Compare official odometer delta with accepted GPS distance.
- Clearly label GPS as estimate/evidence.
- Show quality/degraded context when weak signal or rejected data affects it.
- Near-zero odometer after meaningful accepted GPS movement requires review.
- GPS never fills a missing official odometer automatically.

Layer 5: advisory local calibration.

- GPS permission enables transparent, local, vehicle-specific calibration after
  enough trusted and reviewed same-vehicle evidence.
- Calibration affects only future GPS estimates.
- It never changes confirmed odometer history, mileage allocation, or a user
  record.
- Poor, interrupted, stale, mixed-vehicle, or inconsistent evidence is excluded.
- Consistent offset can suggest tire/speedometer review. Mixed drift directions
  must not create a false recommendation.

GPS SESSION AND DISTANCE ENGINE
-------------------------------
Implemented/verified foundation:

- One active local session is enforced.
- Duplicate starts, callbacks, samples, revisions, handoffs, and completion
  attempts have suppression/rejection behavior.
- Provider-readiness, delayed registration, startup cancellation, restart,
  heartbeat, degraded state, provider loss, and interruption paths have focused
  regression coverage.
- Cached/future location cannot create a current automatic-start proposal.
- Walking evidence does not create vehicle mileage.
- Stationary behavior does not automatically end a trip.
- GPS distance has accepted, estimated-gap, and rejected evidence categories.
- Signal gaps do not fabricate a continuous route.

Lifecycle state model that must remain protected:

- Disabled.
- Awaiting provider registration.
- Awaiting fresh usable initial fix.
- Tracking.
- Degraded tracking.
- User-paused.
- Completion pending.
- Review pending.
- Completed/cancelled terminal state.

Every state transition must carry stable session ID, callback/operation ID,
monotonic/revision evidence, persisted state, and idempotence guard. Wall clock
time is for display; it is not enough by itself to prove ordering.

Provider and location requirements:

- Immediate registration works.
- Delayed registration works.
- Never-registering provider times out to recoverable state.
- Late provider registration after timeout/cancel cannot create a duplicate trip.
- Duplicate provider callbacks cannot create another session.
- Restart while waiting recovers known state without duplication.
- A fresh initial fix is required. Cached, stale, future, invalid-coordinate,
  impossible-jump, implausible-speed, duplicate, or out-of-order evidence is
  rejected or quarantined.
- A pause, rejected sample, long gap, provider change without continuity,
  tunnel-like gap, or clock anomaly cannot fabricate mileage.

Bluetooth vehicle identification
--------------------------------
Implemented/verified foundation:

- Bluetooth is opt-in and initially disabled.
- A local device association can suggest a likely vehicle.
- Bluetooth cannot silently change the vehicle of an active/completed trip.
- Permission denial, permission revocation, retry, prompt disposal, disconnect,
  reconnect, stale observation, repeated broadcasts, storage failure, manual
  start race, and active-session ownership have focused tests.
- Bluetooth connected without movement cannot create an automatic-start proposal.
- Production bootstrap/runtime/heartbeat/Dashboard wiring tests passed together:
  17 tests, exit code 0.

Rules that must remain true:

- Bluetooth disabled never disables manual tracking.
- Unknown/stale/malformed/ambiguous device match fails to manual vehicle choice.
- Multiple known devices must resolve deterministically; ambiguity means no
  silent vehicle assignment.
- Rename of a device must not silently take ownership.
- Connection before application bootstrap is handled safely after initialization.
- Active trip retains confirmed vehicle unless user explicitly changes context
  through a protected vehicle/profile handoff flow.
- Bluetooth is corroboration, never proof of a business trip.

Automatic Evidence Capture / missed-start assistance
----------------------------------------------------
Implemented/verified foundation:

- Automatic evidence capture is opt-in and proposal-only.
- It requires current time-spaced movement, usable accuracy, enough movement
  observations, and independent automotive evidence.
- Independent automotive evidence may be automotive activity recognition or one
  unambiguous associated Bluetooth vehicle.
- GPS-only evidence, one location point, stationary Bluetooth connection, stale
  evidence, future evidence, active/recoverable session, and unknown local
  recovery state fail closed.
- Proposal cannot invent starting odometer, business purpose, job, mileage
  classification, final TripLog entry, or confirmed stop.
- Free plan policy is four accepted recoveries per monthly period.
- Detection, proposal display, dismissal, rejection, and expiration consume no
  allowance. Only accepted eligible proposal uses an allowance.
- Paid users bypass quota only; approval remains mandatory.
- Focused automatic detector/coordinator/allowance suite passed: 16 tests.

Required user-facing explanation:

- What the optional feature observes.
- Why it observes it: to stage a review if a workday may have been missed.
- What it does not do: no silent day/trip/business classification/odometer edit.
- Permissions potentially needed and how to turn it off.
- That the user has final approval for every proposed entry.

Proposal review requirements:

- Proposed/review-needed entries should be visually distinct from user-entered
  confirmed entries, for example orange versus green after confirmation.
- Do not display raw confidence percentages. Explain evidence in plain English.
- Show time range, suggested vehicle only when unambiguous, evidence summary,
  and no-change assurance.
- User can review now, defer, reject, or disable assistance.
- Review allows vehicle/profile confirmation, physical odometer, classification,
  correction/backdate context, and optional TripLog handoff.

Future patterns and schedules:

- User-approved regular work patterns may improve proposal ordering or wording.
- Patterns are optional, local, explainable, removable, and never confirm a
  record.
- Scheduled-stop assistance may eventually combine confirmed schedule, address,
  GPS distance/accuracy, dwell, route sequence, Bluetooth, motion, and recurring
  history. “Same street” is not sufficient logic.
- Raw GPS does not create a job or invoice. A confirmed job may later be used by
  an invoice, preserving clean ownership.

Vehicle-use allocation and cost-percentage foundation
-----------------------------------------------------
Implemented/verified foundation:

- Confirmed mileage is classified business, personal, split, or unclassified.
- Unclassified is retained and is never silently treated as business.
- Split mileage uses exact integer tenths.
- Allocation record includes vehicle, source type/id, source revision, occurred
  time, confirmed time, use, total tenths, business/personal/unclassified
  tenths.
- Duplicate revision cannot add mileage twice.
- Newer revision replaces older revision.
- Stale revision cannot overwrite newer confirmation.
- Equal conflicting revision is excluded rather than selected by input order.
- A real collision defect was found and repaired: colon-containing valid IDs
  could produce the same joined source key across distinct vehicle/source
  combinations. Source identities are now length-prefixed/collision-safe.
- Expanded allocation/consent/read-model/exact-tenth/stress suite passed 27
  tests after this repair.

Expense percentage contract:

- This feature is optional.
- No opt-in: no suggested percentage.
- No confirmed mileage: no percentage.
- Any unclassified mileage: review required and no percentage.
- Complete opted-in period: expose exact business numerator and classified
  denominator as well as a display percentage.
- Expense may consume this as a suggestion only after user review.
- This system does not create, edit, categorize, or approve expenses, receipts,
  fuel records, insurance, vehicle payment, maintenance, or taxes.

Localization and units

Implemented/verified foundation:

Dashboard functional requirements
---------------------------------
Dashboard projects evidence; it does not own records.

Pre-workday gig/delivery Dashboard must project:

- Default/selected vehicle and official odometer.
- Default/selected work profile.
- Tracking readiness: permission, background capability, provider state,
  Bluetooth state if enabled, and action-needed warning.
- Review Inbox count.
- Current day or recent summary where relevant.
- Clear Start Day action that is not a giant distracting round control.
- Access to configured quick actions and Dashboard navigation.

Active-day gig/delivery Dashboard must project:

- Active vehicle/work profile without redundant repeated labels.
- Official live odometer and clearly separate advisory GPS estimate.
- Tenths where known, without false precision.
- Shift timer and day state.
- GPS/provider/background/Bluetooth health where enabled.
- Pending odometer reviews and proposal count.
- Expense/payment/invoice summary projections supplied by their owners.
- Manual stop/pickup/drop-off/note paths.
- Compact reachable Pause/End Day actions.
- Calendar access as a projection/navigation requirement, without Calendar record
  ownership being taken into this system.

Post-workday Dashboard must project:

- Completed versus review-pending day state.
- Odometer start/end separate from GPS evidence.
- Classification totals/unresolved warning.
- References to expenses/payments/invoices/receipts/stops/proposals owned by
  their respective modules.
- Clear “Review Workday” destination.

Contractor Dashboard must be distinct:

- It must support driver and office/owner views.
- It will eventually project schedule/jobs/route/workday evidence, vehicle and
  team context where applicable, document status, review queue, and operational
  health.
- It cannot directly own job, calendar, invoice, or payment records.

Start Day flow requirements:

1. Ask user to select work profile only if more than one valid profile exists.
2. Ask user to select vehicle only if more than one valid vehicle exists.
3. If only one option applies, preselect it visibly and allow change.
4. Ask for physical current odometer; keypad must not dominate screen first.
5. Run continuity/review checks.
6. Create exactly one Workday Evidence Session.
7. Back means go back/cancel, never confirmation.
8. Navigate to active day deterministically; no blank black transition screen.

End Day flow requirements:

1. Safely stop/review active GPS trip before generic ending odometer entry.
2. Ask physical ending odometer.
3. Run continuity/history/GPS/allocation review as appropriate.
4. Preserve lower-reading review; never silently close/erase it.
5. Project Workday Integrity Summary.
6. Require explicit confirmation/handoff.
7. Return to correct Dashboard state without blank intermediary screen.

Every secondary screen needs predictable Back behavior and platform navigation
gesture support where available. Active actions must route to actual functions;
inactive controls must be visibly unavailable rather than pretending to work.

Operational health panel requirements:

- Review Queue.
- GPS Health.
- Bluetooth Health.
- Backup/Sync status.
- Tracking readiness.
- Outstanding issues.
- Incomplete workday status.

Use plain language and a next action, not meaningless raw error codes.

Security and privacy requirements
---------------------------------
The app is local-first and privacy-first.

Routine GPS health telemetry must never contain raw coordinates, route geometry,
customer name/address, job/invoice/payment/receipt/OCR contents, photographs,
API keys, tokens, credentials, or raw native device payloads. It should use
aggregate counts, safe reason codes, quality bands, state transitions, permission
categories, Bluetooth result categories, proposal outcome, and aggregate
GPS-to-odometer discrepancy bucket.

Any future owner/developer Command Center must consume safe aggregate health
information, not use the app as a way to inspect a person’s private route or
business data.

Local integrity requirements:

- Preserve originals plus correction audit, never destructive silent overwrite.
- Reject duplicates, stale revisions, malformed IDs, invalid timestamps,
  negative distances, invalid totals, impossible sequence/order, and corrupt
  storage records.
- Recover conservatively from corrupt newer snapshots using older valid local
  evidence only where proven safe, then surface review.
- Partial writes/interrupted completion leave recoverable state.

Backup/sync boundary:

- Local validated records remain operational truth.
- Remote counter cannot authorize sync by itself.
- Free sync requires validated local reservation/usage before upload.
- Failed/blocked reservation cannot consume quota or delete local data.
- Reservation success is not mirror-write success.
- Mirror success needs validated acknowledgement.
- Remote backup cannot alter official odometer, overwrite local daytime truth,
  create trip/stop/classification, purge local data, or upload raw routes by
  default.

Current sync policy repair
--------------------------
Root cause found:

- Authoritative local hosted fallback says four free syncs per rolling 24-hour
  window.
- Several GPS-side tests, safe summaries, and one reservation summary validator
  hard-coded six.
- This mismatch was responsible for the prior complete gate’s shared entitlement
  failures after it had reached 1,978 tests.

Repair completed and verified:

- Make local GPS/settings safe summaries and validation derive from one shared
  hosted limit rather than magic number six.
- Update stale tests to verify the authoritative configuration.
- Do not modify Firebase backend, durable storage schema, remote grants, backup
  transport, or server implementation.
- Focused sync policy, guard, reservation, and backup-scope tests passed: 40
  tests, exit code 0.
- The complete trip-tracking QA gate then passed 1,990 tests, exit code 0.

QA and stress architecture
--------------------------
One reusable deterministic shared harness must serve smoke 1,000, routine
10,000, development stress 100,000, extended 500,000, and deep 1,000,000
scenarios. It must not create copied suites and must preserve existing Inventory
and Work Supplies harness consumers.

Harness rules:

- Incremental generation in bounded batches.
- Discard successful traces.
- Retain detailed trace only for failure.
- Record seed, scenario index, parameters, configuration, version, Git revision,
  elapsed time, scenarios per second, invalid/skipped/failure counts, memory.
- Same seed/configuration must reproduce same sequence and digest.
- A deliberate failure-injection regression must prove replay works.
- Do not print one line per successful scenario.
- Avoid retaining every route/sample/event in memory.

Required coverage families:

- Provider immediate/delayed/never/late/duplicate/timeout/cancel/recovery.
- Fresh/stale/inaccurate/approximate/revoked/out-of-order/duplicate/invalid GPS.
- Stationary/walking/crawling/highway/stops/dwell/pickup/delivery/fuel/rest.
- Foreground/background/restart/process death/OS kill/interrupted persistence.
- Heartbeat freshness/degraded/recovery/provider loss/clock/time-zone change.
- Normal/low/critical battery, charging/unplugging, saver/quality-mode changes.
- Correct/wrong/multiple/stale/renamed Bluetooth; deny/revoke/reconnect/races.
- Zero/short/long/mixed/rejected/estimated distance, calibration, exact tenths.
- Allocation/classification/revision/collision/vehicle/profile boundaries.
- Duplicate session/revision/handoff, corrupt snapshot, completion pending.

Existing stress evidence:

- 100,000 scenarios with seed 72720260801 passed.
- Exact repeat with same seed produced same digest and passed.
- A different seed 81820260801 passed.
- Zero failures, invalid scenarios, or skips in those runs.
- Successful traces were bounded; observed peak memory approximately 241–253 MB.

This is simulation evidence only. It does not prove actual GPS accuracy or
background behavior in a vehicle.

conditions, durable recovery proof, accessible and understandable flows, privacy
review, known limitations documented, health monitoring that does not expose
private user data, and no unresolved integrity defect.
