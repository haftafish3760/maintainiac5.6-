Calendar requirements and follow-up notes

The shared calendar day screen is the operational timeline for the owner,
employee, contractor, and app-specific modules.

Must preserve:
- Every calendar day needs previous-day and next-day navigation.
- The date should appear once in the day navigation row, not duplicated in the
  page title/header.
- Employee and contractor calendar days must show which vehicle or vehicles
  were used that day.
- Vehicle use can be plural. An employee may start in one vehicle and switch to
  another during the same day.
- Day entries must stay chronological when timestamps exist.
- Entries without a timestamp should use entry-created order, newest first.
- Calendar day recap must include every important operational metric the user
  can reasonably need for record keeping.
- Recaps eventually need miles, business miles, personal miles, MPG, fuel cost,
  cost per mile, revenue per mile, expenses, invoices/payments, gross pay,
  materials used, receipts, maintenance, repairs, and profit where permissions
  allow it.
- Employees should only see recap fields their permissions allow.
- Owners and authorized managers can correct employee entries, hours, expenses,
  vehicle assignments, materials, and job links.
- Corrections that affect pay, money, records, or permissions must eventually
  create audit events.
- No VINs, plates, passenger data, patient data, or other unnecessary private
  data should be placed in cloud-backed calendar records.

Current UI note:
- The active vehicle display in the employee calendar is preview-only until the
  real vehicle/day assignment model is wired.
