Source: visible user messages, 2026-07-26 Receipt Assist and Expenses workflow

- Expenses serves contractors and gig drivers and must retain the active work
  profile so its dashboard, defaults, categories, and receipt suggestions can
  fit the work the user is doing. A user may keep more than one work profile.
- Receipt Assist has three user-selectable detail levels: Total Only, Category
  Summary, and Detailed Items. The default is an Expenses receipt setting and
  the receipt form must provide a clear contextual override.
- Total Only captures the final amount and Business, Personal, or Split. Split
  accepts either a dollar amount or percentage and derives the remaining side.
- Category Summary adds a receipt-level category without requiring purchased
  item lines.
- Detailed Items faithfully reconstructs the printed merchant/contact details,
  date/time, totals, and every purchased-item row found by OCR. Each editable
  item supports printed description, quantity, unit price, line total, and
  review state without inventing an exact product identity.
- Detailed receipts ask whether one category applies to the entire receipt. If
  yes, category is chosen once at receipt level. If no, every item can choose
  its own category and Business, Personal, or Split allocation.
- Manual entry uses the same forms and must always expose Add Item in Detailed
  Items mode. OCR suggestions never remove manual correction, split/merge, add,
  or delete controls.
- Receipt progress uses one concise, bounded status surface. It must identify
  the actual current stage, finish or offer manual recovery within the device
  deadline, and never show a completed step as still spinning.
- Receipt forms are responsive from small phones through tablets and desktop
  windows, respect accessibility text scaling, and never clip or ellipsize
  instructional sentences.
- Expense categories need consistent matching icons and broad coverage for
  contractor and gig-driver records. Category-specific fields should appear
  only when relevant; unrelated category forms must not be combined merely
  because they share the Expenses section.
- PDF receipt import remains part of the receipt system, but camera capture,
  local OCR extraction, editable review, and manual receipt entry must be
  completed and proven before PDF-specific polish.
